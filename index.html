LeafDoc

Smartphone plant imaging system with AI-powered diagnosis

  • SolidWorks
  • 3D Printing
  • Kotlin
  • CameraX
  • Image Stitching

What

  • A $50 handheld lightbox that attaches to a smartphone, blocking ambient light and providing uniform LED illumination.
  • Contoured rollers flatten and center the leaf on entry, giving repeatable positioning with no manual adjustment.
  • MagSafe mounting locks the phone above the sample in a fixed, repeatable position.

How

  • Modeled and iterated through 7 enclosure revisions in SolidWorks, 3D printing each to test light leak, roller clearance, and component fit.
  • Wrote an alignment stitching algorithm that assembles overlapping frames into a single full-length leaf image.
  • Built a native Android app (Kotlin, CameraX) with manual camera controls and a multi-provider AI diagnostic pipeline.

Results

  • Users capture, organize, and manage imaging sessions entirely within the app.
  • Cloud-based AI returns a health score, primary finding, and confidence level in-app — no export or external tools needed.

FluroScan

Portable photosynthetic efficiency imaging system

  • Raspberry Pi
  • SolidWorks
  • Optics
  • LED Design

What

  • Direct measurement of photosynthetic efficiency via chlorophyll fluorescence.
  • Real-time, non-invasive imaging that detects plant stress early.
  • A more affordable and accessible alternative to existing instrumentation.

How

  • Prototyped the enclosure in SolidWorks and 3D printed on Bambu Lab hardware.
  • Used a Raspberry Pi for camera and system control.
  • Dark-adapted leaves to fully open PSII, saturated the photosystems with 450 nm LEDs, and captured emission through a 680/720 nm filter.

Theory

  • A weak, non-actinic light excites and opens PSII reaction centers, giving baseline fluorescence (Fo).
  • A strong pulse closes all PSII reaction centers, giving maximum fluorescence (Fm).
  • The ratio Fv/Fm, where Fv = Fm − Fo, represents the maximum efficiency of PSII energy conversion.

Specifications

  • 450 nm excitation at 4000 µmol m−2s−1, using 12 LEDs in a 3-up configuration on an angled mount.

Multispectral Handheld Imaging Device

Field-portable plant phenotyping instrument

  • Raspberry Pi
  • Electronics
  • Soldering
  • Reverse Engineering

What

  • A plant phenotyping device that collects high-resolution images at 0.04 mm/pixel.
  • Redesigned the enclosure to house all electronics internally, eliminating external wiring for a fully field-portable system.

How

  • Prototyped in SolidWorks and 3D printed on Bambu Lab hardware; Raspberry Pi for camera and system control.
  • Reverse-engineered and documented all subsystems of the original design.
  • Soldered 120+ LEDs across 6 wavelength bands with MOSFET transistors and 12V/5V power conversion.

Results

  • High signal-to-noise ratio with ambient light fully blocked; secondary leaf veins clearly resolved.
  • Detected plant stress days before visual symptoms appeared.
  • Enabled a co-researcher's ML model to reach 90% classification accuracy in a peer-reviewed publication (co-author).
